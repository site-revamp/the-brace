The Brace Orthodontic Practice Website Redesign
1. Website Overview
The Brace Orthodontic Practice is a specialist orthodontic clinic based in Central London, providing private orthodontic treatments for adults, teenagers and children.

The practice specialises in Invisalign, clear aligners, fixed braces and advanced orthodontic solutions, supported by digital treatment monitoring and personalised care.

The redesigned homepage focuses on positioning The Brace as a specialist orthodontic provider with expertise in complex Invisalign cases and modern teeth-straightening treatments. It targets patients seeking discreet orthodontic solutions, parents exploring treatment for children and individuals requiring specialist orthodontic care.

The primary conversion objective is to encourage visitors to book a free orthodontic consultation.
2. Main Improvements
1. Clearer Orthodontic Treatment Presentation
- Improved the organisation of Invisalign, B-Aligned clear aligners, metal braces, ceramic braces and lingual braces.
- Created clearer distinctions between adult, teenage and children's orthodontic treatments.
- Strengthened the visibility of specialist orthodontic services and complex treatment capabilities.
- Addresses the challenge of helping visitors understand the available treatment options and identify the most suitable orthodontic solution.
2. Stronger Specialist Positioning and Clinical Credibility
- Refined the homepage messaging to emphasise specialist orthodontists and the practice's Invisalign expertise.
- Highlighted key differentiators, including Invisalign Apex Provider status, Dental Monitoring technology and combined specialist experience.
- Strengthened the presentation of the practice's experience with complex orthodontic cases.
- Addresses the need to communicate the practice's specialist expertise and distinguish it from general dental providers offering orthodontic treatments.
3. Improved Consultation and Conversion Journey
- Strengthened the visibility of the free initial orthodontic consultation as the primary conversion opportunity.
- Improved the content flow connecting treatment information with consultation enquiries.
- Gave clearer attention to supporting decision-making information, including treatment options, flexible payment arrangements and patient suitability.
- Addresses the challenge of guiding prospective patients from exploring orthodontic treatments towards taking the next step.
3. Design Improvements
The redesigned homepage introduces a more refined and specialist-focused visual presentation aligned with The Brace's orthodontic positioning.

Key improvements include:
- Visual presentation: A more professional appearance reflecting specialist orthodontic care and modern dental technology.
- Layout: Better organisation of treatment categories, clinical expertise and consultation information.
- Typography: Clearer heading hierarchy and improved readability across homepage sections.
- Consistency: More cohesive presentation of orthodontic services, specialist credentials and brand messaging.
- User experience: A more logical browsing journey for adults, teenagers and parents exploring treatment options.
- Conversion focus: Greater prominence for the free consultation and clearer progression towards patient enquiries.

5. Project Objective
The objective of the homepage redesign is to strengthen The Brace's positioning as a specialist orthodontic practice and improve the journey from treatment discovery to consultation booking.

The updated direction prioritises specialist credibility, treatment clarity, patient-focused communication and consultation accessibility while highlighting the practice's expertise in Invisalign, clear aligners and advanced orthodontic care.
