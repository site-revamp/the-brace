A more considered approach

Orthodontic treatment is personal. Your goals, your bite, your lifestyle and your clinical needs all matter when deciding what treatment is right for you.

Discover your patient journey →

Not just straighter teeth. A treatment plan that starts with you.

The Brace brings specialist orthodontic knowledge together with modern treatment options, from clear aligners to fixed and lingual braces. The aim is to help you understand your choices and feel confident about the next step.

Explore your options

Orthodontic treatment, thoughtfully matched to you.

Different smiles need different solutions. Explore the treatment approaches available at The Brace and discuss suitability with a specialist orthodontist.

Fixed braces

Metal and ceramic options for a range of orthodontic needs, planned around your bite and treatment goals.

Clear aligners

Discreet, removable aligner options, including Invisalign and the practice's B-Aligned offering.

Teen & child care

Orthodontic assessment and treatment options for younger patients, including Invisalign First where suitable.

Lingual braces

A discreet fixed-brace approach positioned behind the teeth, subject to individual clinical suitability.

Dental monitoring

Remote monitoring technology can support aspects of treatment follow-up between in-practice appointments.

Smile finishing

Ask about hygiene, whitening and other complementary services offered by the practice.

The right treatment depends on a clinical assessment. Your orthodontist can explain suitable options, expected steps and any limitations before you decide.
